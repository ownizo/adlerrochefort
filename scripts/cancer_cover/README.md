# Cancer cover pages in every language — sources

Content for `scripts/build-cancer-cover-pages.py`. One module per language tree
(`pt`, `en`, `de`, `es`, `nl`, `fr`, `it`, `pl`, `se`, `dk`, `il`, `zh`), each with
`PAGE`. UI strings (meta line, author box, form labels, service-language note) come
from `scripts/ipmi_intl/<lang>.py`.

Every fact is one already published on the site, stated by the owner, or taken from
the insurer document listed below. Nothing else may be added without a source.

- Portuguese insurers generally exclude pre-existing conditions and do not accept new
  members over 65; MGEN is the only exception (pre-existing conditions after a
  365-day waiting period, no medical questionnaire, no upper entry age, two or more
  insured people from the same household, Portuguese NIF and address with proof):
  owner, October 2026; `/seguros/saude/` and
  `/en/blog/mgen-medis-allianz-april-acceptance-comparison/`.
- Médis top option (Opção 3): Doenças Graves cover of EUR 1,000,000, 180-day waiting
  period for Doenças Graves, oncology prostheses/orthoses and assistance services
  (psychological support, home nursing), unlimited second opinion, pre-existing
  conditions excluded: Médis proposal form, April 2025
  (medis.pt/media/7572/proposta-individuais-pt_medis-editavel.pdf). Placed through
  the agency agreement with Zurich: published on the comparison article above.
  Owner: Médis has good capitals.
- Cigna Global: cancer care in the core cover of Silver, Gold and Platinum, paid in
  full; Silver annual limit US$1,000,000 (cignaglobal.com); Platinum highest annual
  limit without a fixed cap, worldwide excl./incl. USA, US citizens accepted:
  `/en/international-health-insurance/cigna-global/`.
- Allianz Care: hospital treatment in every plan, cancer screenings across the range,
  Europe area alongside worldwide options, no residents of Brazil:
  `/en/international-health-insurance/allianz-care/` and Allianz Partners, November
  2025.
- IPMI is for people who want to carry on with their life and be treated wherever
  they are: owner, October 2026.

The ES page is written for Latin American clients moving to Portugal or Spain (owner
decision), not translated from the EN page. NL, FR, IT, PL, SE, DK, IL and ZH carry
the same "service in English" note as the IPMI pages.
