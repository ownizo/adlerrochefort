Um welches Bedürfnis es meist geht

Kaum jemand beginnt mit „ich will eine Lebensversicherung“ — sie beginnen mit einem konkreten Anliegen, und die passende Form folgt daraus.

  • Familienschutz. Einkommen ersetzen oder eine Summe für Angehörige, wenn Sie nicht mehr da wären.
  • Mortgage protection. Deckung, begrifflich an eine offene Immobilienverbindlichkeit gebunden — sieheHypothekenschutz in Spanien for the dedicated page.
  • Income replacement. Covering ongoing household costs, not just a one-off debt.
  • Debt protection. Beyond a mortgage — other significant borrowing a family would otherwise be left carrying.
  • Business or family obligations. Eine Gesellschaftervereinbarung, ein Familienkredit oder eine ähnliche Abrede mit Lebensversicherungskomponente.
  • Liquidity considerations. Nur im Überblick — wo eine Summe im Todesfall einer Familie hilft, Kosten oder Pflichten zu tragen, die dann entstehen. Das ist keine Steuer- oder Nachlassberatung.

Risikolebensversicherung, konzeptionell

Das Kernprodukt, das die meisten mit „Lebensversicherung“ meinen, zahlt eine festgelegte Summe, wenn Sie innerhalb einer festgelegten Laufzeit sterben — eine klare Idee, Versicherer strukturieren die Details aber unterschiedlich.

Wenn Sie bereits Lebensversicherung aus einem anderen Land haben

Ein Umzug nach Spanien heißt nicht automatisch, dass Ihre bestehende UK-, EU- oder andere internationale Lebenspolice aufhört zu greifen — und wir raten nicht zur Kündigung, bevor Sie das geprüft haben.

  • Territorial scope. Whether the policy responds the same way once you live outside the country it was written in.
  • Residency conditions. Some policies have conditions tied to where the policyholder is resident.
  • Policy terms. Whether anything else in the wording assumes a specific country of residence.
  • Currency. Whether the sum insured is fixed in a currency that still makes sense for your situation.
  • Begünstigte. Whether they are still correctly named and reachable.
  • Continuation rules. Whether the policy has its own rules about what happens on a move abroad.

We generalise only this far. The actual answer for your policy is in its wording — send it to us and we will tell you plainly what it says, rather than guess based on what is typical.

Häufige Fragen

Do I need Lebensversicherung in Spanien?

Not universally — it depends on your circumstances. It tends to matter most where others depend on your income, where a mortgage or debt would otherwise fall to your family, or where a business or family obligation is tied to it. We work through this with you rather than assume it.

Can I keep my UK or other foreign life insurance policy after moving to Spain?

Often, yes — but check its territorial scope, residency conditions and currency with the insurer that issued it before assuming it continues to work exactly as before. We do not recommend cancelling an existing policy before you have actually confirmed its terms.

How much life insurance do I need?

There is no universal formula. It depends on outstanding debts, dependants, household income, existing savings and any cover you already hold — we work through this with you rather than apply a generic multiplier.

Will you ask for my medical history?

Not through this form. We ask for the basics needed to understand your situation; any medical questions an insurer needs answered are handled directly with the insurer, as part of underwriting.

Can you advise on inheritance tax or succession planning?

No. We are an insurance intermediary, not a tax or legal adviser. Where a question touches Spanish succession or tax treatment, we say so and point you towards a qualified specialist.

Do you offer investment-linked or savings life insurance?

Not currently. This page covers protection-focused life insurance — cover in the event of death — not investment or savings products.
