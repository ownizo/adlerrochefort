Who we are

How we work

  • Quotes, the explanation of the conditions, correspondence and claims support are in English, in writing, so you can read back everything that was agreed.
  • The policy itself is issued by the insurer in Portuguese or Spanish. Before you sign, we tell you in writing what the sums insured, conditions and exclusions mean for you.
  • We reply within 24 working hours. No call or meeting is needed at any stage.
  • A broker is normally paid by the insurer through commission built into the premium, not a separate fee charged to you. We confirm this for your case before anything is arranged.
Lines of cover

What we insure

  • International health insurance with Allianz Care and Cigna Global, for expats in Portugal, Spain or anywhere else: international health insurance.
  • Private health insurance in Portugal and Spain, including the cover needed for residence visas.
  • High-value homes in Portugal and Spain, with Chubb, Hiscox and Liberty, and second homes that stand empty part of the year.
  • Art, jewellery and watches on agreed value: art, jewellery and watch insurance.
  • Family liability, car and life and mortgage protection: family liability in Spain, car insurance in Portugal, mortgage protection in Spain.
  • Landlords letting long-term or to holidaymakers: Portugal, Spain.

Portugal and Spain together

Many of our clients have a home, a family member or a business on both sides of the border. Chubb and Hiscox write both countries, so homes and valuables in Portugal and in Spain can often be insured with the same adviser and aligned conditions, instead of two unrelated policies in two languages.

We work online and in writing, so it makes no difference where in Portugal or Spain you live: Lisbon, Cascais, the Algarve, the Costa del Sol, the Balearics, the Costa Blanca, Madrid or Barcelona.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is everything really in English?

Quotes, the explanation of the conditions, correspondence and claims support are in English, in writing. The policy document itself is issued by the insurer in Portuguese or Spanish, and we explain it before you sign.

Are you registered to arrange insurance in Portugal and Spain?

Yes. Adler & Rochefort (Ownizo, Unipessoal Lda.) is registered with Portugal's ASF under no. 425591790/3 and places Spanish risks under the EU freedom to provide services.

Are you tied to one insurer?

No. We compare within the insurer relationships we hold, such as Allianz Care, Cigna Global, APRIL International, Chubb, Hiscox and Liberty, and say plainly when something falls outside them.

Does using a broker cost extra?

A broker is normally paid by the insurer through commission built into the premium, not a separate fee charged to you. We confirm this for your case before anything is arranged.

Do I need to meet you or call you?

No. We work in writing and reply within 24 working hours. If you prefer to talk, you can also write to us on WhatsApp.
