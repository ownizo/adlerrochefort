Insurance for South Africans in Portugal

Relocating, retiring or investing in Portugal from South Africa? Several things you rely on at home do not carry over: medical aid stops at the border, there is no S1 or EHIC route, your licence and no-claims record need checking, and your home is insured in euros at its rebuild value. We explain it in writing, in English.

What the law requires

South Africa is outside EU social-security co-ordination, so there is no S1 form and no European Health Insurance Card. Access to the Portuguese SNS comes with legal residence and registration at your local health centre; until then, private cover is your cover.

Portuguese health insurers issue policies to people with a residential address in Portugal and a Portuguese NIF for every insured person. For the D7 or D8 visa application itself, consulates usually ask for travel insurance with medical cover.

A South African licence is not among those Portugal recognises under its simplified OECD/CPLP regime; as a resident you will normally need to exchange it at the IMT. A South African no-claims record does not transfer automatically.

The mistakes we see most often

Resigning from medical aid before Portuguese cover starts

The gap between arrival and a Portuguese policy can be weeks. New cover first, resignation second.

Insuring the house at a converted purchase price

The right figure is the rebuild value in euros, reviewed every year.

Driving on a licence that is no longer valid for residents

Check the exchange route and deadline with the IMT as soon as you are resident.

Leaving security measures undeclared

Alarms and safes help only if the policy knows about them — and they must be working on the day.

Frequently asked questions

Does my medical aid cover me once I live in Portugal?

Not as resident cover. See medical aid and health insurance in Portugal.

Is there an S1 or EHIC route for South Africans?

No. Those are EU mechanisms. Public healthcare comes with residence and SNS registration.

Can I keep driving on my South African licence?

As a resident you will normally need to exchange it at the IMT. Confirm the deadline and whether a test applies in your case.

Do you handle everything in English?

Yes. Quotes, policy explanations, correspondence and claims, in writing. Portuguese policies are issued in Portuguese; we make sure you understand them before you sign.
