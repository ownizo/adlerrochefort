Our team

Who is by your side

Hugo Gonçalves

Founder & Risk Management Specialist

Hugo Gonçalves is the driving force behind Adler & Rochefort, combining extensive international experience with a unique specialisation in insurance consultancy for expats and residents in Portugal. His vision is clear: to give private clients and their families risk management that transcends the traditional policy.

With a degree in Business Management from the University of Hertfordshire and ongoing training at the Chartered Insurance Institute (CII), Hugo works from a technical grounding rather than a sales one. Nearly twenty years at the forefront of global tourism technology at companies such as Hotelbeds (HBX Group) gave him a working knowledge of how international clients hold assets across several countries, and of what that does to a policy written in one of them.

Since founding Adler & Rochefort, Hugo has been redefining the role of the insurance intermediary in Portugal. He has focused on cultivating strong partnerships with leading insurers such as Allianz, April, Hiscox and Zurich, and on giving English-speaking clients clear, independent advice. The result is access to the markets that will underwrite a risk individually, from health and home insurance to policies for private clients with assets the retail market will not take.

His working method is to read the policy before recommending it, and to say plainly where the cover stops. Each client's needs are analysed in detail, cover is built around what they actually own and where it is kept, and everything is done under ASF supervision.

An insurance intermediary is a professional authorised by the ASF who independently analyses the market to find the best protection solutions for your case. Unlike an agent tied to a single insurer, an independent intermediary works for you — comparing products, negotiating terms and supporting you in claims management, always defending your interests.

Often yes, but it depends on the insurer and product. Health insurance for a visa or relocation can usually be started with passport details, while car, mortgage or home insurance may require a NIF, Portuguese address, bank details or property documentation before the policy can be issued.
Portuguese private health insurers normally assess declared medical history before accepting cover. A condition may be excluded, accepted after a waiting period, accepted with limits or priced differently. The safest approach is to disclose it clearly and compare insurers before applying.
