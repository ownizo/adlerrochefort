Insuring a high-value home in Comporta & Melides

Between the rice fields, pine forest and dunes of the Alentejo coast, Comporta and Melides have become one of Europe's most sought-after second-home destinations — and one of the more particular places to insure a house.

Our working language is English. Quotes, explanation of terms, correspondence and claims are all handled in English. Portuguese insurers issue their policies in Portuguese and Spanish insurers in Spanish; what we add is that you understand exactly what they say before you sign. All communication is in writing.

What is different in Comporta & Melides

Timber, thatch and pine forest

Many houses use timber structures and thatched roofs, surrounded by pine woodland. Insurers look closely at construction materials, the distance to vegetation and fire protection before accepting the risk.

Homes that stand empty

Most houses here are lived in for part of the year. What changes in the policy after a number of days without anyone in the house must be read for each contract.

Building and renovating

New builds and major renovations need construction cover during the works, and the home policy has to be in place on the day the house is handed over.

What we arrange in Comporta & Melides

High-value homes

The house, its contents and valuables, with the sum insured based on rebuild cost.

Art, jewellery & watches

Listed items on an agreed value, at home and when travelling.

Family liability

Limits in line with the household's assets — for guests, staff and every home.

Frequently asked questions

Do you arrange cover for homes in Comporta & Melides?

Yes. Adler & Rochefort is registered in Portugal with the ASF (no. 425591790/3) and in Spain with the DGSFP (LPS). Cover is placed with specialist insurers, subject to their acceptance of the risk and to the policy wording.

Is there a minimum value?

Each specialist insurer sets its own entry level, based on the rebuild cost, the contents and the valuables. Below that, a well-built standard policy is often the better answer, and we say so in writing.

Is earthquake cover included?

Not automatically. In Portugal earthquake cover is an option that has to be added to the home policy, usually with its own excess. Whether it makes sense depends on the property and its location.
