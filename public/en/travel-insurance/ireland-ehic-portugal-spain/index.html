Irish travellers: EHIC or travel insurance?

Travelling from Ireland: what the EHIC covers in Portugal and Spain — and what it does not

Irish residents carry the European Health Insurance Card and many assume it is enough for a week in the Algarve or a month at a villa in Spain. It is a useful card, not a travel policy.

Comparison service · Policies sold by HelloSafe (ORIAS 21008038) · Reviewed 21 November 2026

Compare premium plans

What the EHIC does

The EHIC gives access to state healthcare in Portugal and Spain on the same terms as local residents — including any patient charges. It is accepted in public hospitals and health centres.

What the EHIC does not do

  • It does not pay for private hospitals and clinics, where many tourist areas have their emergency care.
  • It does not pay for repatriation to Ireland.
  • It does not cover mountain rescue, cancellation, baggage or liability.
  • It is not valid on cruise ships or outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EHIC enough for Portugal?

It covers public healthcare on local terms. For private care, repatriation and cancellation you need travel insurance.

Can I buy travel insurance here as an Irish resident?

Yes. Select Ireland as country of residence — it is preset on this page.

Do I still need the EHIC with travel insurance?

Carry both: many insurers expect you to use public care with the EHIC where appropriate.
