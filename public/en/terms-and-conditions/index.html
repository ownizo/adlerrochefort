5. Fiscal Representation and Recurring Annual Services

In addition to insurance brokerage, Adler & Rochefort coordinates fiscal representation in Portugal and related settle-in services. Fiscal representation is contracted as a recurring annual service, not as a single task. The regulated work is carried out by an independent certified accountant, who is fully responsible for it; Adler & Rochefort acts as intermediary, coordinator and single point of contact, and does not provide tax, accounting or legal advice.

5.1 Price. Fiscal representation is charged at €199 per year, VAT included. Where the service is contracted as part of the Complete settle-in package, the first-year fee is €499, VAT included, and covers the additional onboarding services described on that package; renewal years are charged at €199 per year and cover fiscal representation only. The Corporate package is a separate, non-recurring company formation service priced from €1,200 + VAT, and any ongoing accounting is contracted and billed directly by the certified accountant.

5.2 Duration. The service runs for an initial term of twelve months, beginning on the date the appointment as fiscal representative is accepted before the Portuguese Tax Authority.

5.3 Automatic renewal. At the end of each term the service renews automatically for a further twelve months, on the anniversary of the appointment, at the annual fee then applicable to fiscal representation, for as long as the client remains a non-resident for Portuguese tax purposes.

5.4 Notice before renewal. Adler & Rochefort notifies the client in writing, by email to the address held on file, no less than 30 days before each renewal date. The notice states the renewal date, the fee payable and how to cancel.

5.5 Cancellation. The client may cancel at any time before the renewal date, in writing, with no penalty, by emailing insurance@adlerrochefort.com. Cancellation is confirmed in writing. Where cancellation is received before the renewal date, the service ends at the end of the current term and no further fee is charged. Fees already paid for a term that has begun are not refundable in part, save where refund is required by law.

5.6 Non-payment. Renewal invoices are payable by the renewal date. Where payment is not received, Adler & Rochefort sends a written reminder and allows a further 15 days. If payment is still outstanding after that period, the service may be suspended and the appointment as fiscal representative resigned before the Tax Authority, and the client is notified in writing when this happens. The client remains responsible for appointing a replacement representative, or for using another means of receiving communications from the Tax Authority, where an obligation to do so applies to them.

5.7 Becoming tax resident in Portugal. The service is provided only while the client is a non-resident for Portuguese tax purposes. The client must inform Adler & Rochefort in writing on becoming tax resident in Portugal, and must update their address on the Portal das Finanças. On confirmation, the appointment is ended and the service is not renewed. No renewal fee is charged for a term beginning after the date on which written notice of the change of residence is received.

5.8 Scope. Fiscal representation covers receiving and forwarding communications from the Portuguese Tax Authority and acting as the client's registered contact. It does not include the preparation or submission of tax returns, the payment of taxes on the client's behalf, or representation in inspection, complaint or appeal proceedings, unless separately agreed in writing.
