International health cover when your children are at school abroad

When children live or study outside Portugal, family health insurance becomes more complex. A Portuguese resident policy may work well for parents at home, but a child at boarding school, university or an international campus may need cover that follows them across borders.

Start with where the child actually lives

Insurers distinguish between residence, nationality, school location and temporary travel. A child who spends most of the academic year abroad may not be treated as a short-term traveller. Families should check whether the policy covers routine treatment, emergencies and follow-up care in the school country, not only during holidays.

What school or university cover may miss

Some schools provide basic accident or local medical arrangements, but these may not include private specialist care, mental health support, dental treatment, chronic medication, evacuation, repatriation or treatment during holidays outside the school country. Parents should ask for the wording, not only a certificate of enrolment cover.

Key cover areas for families

  • Outpatient consultations, diagnostics and specialist referrals in the school country.
  • Emergency hospitalisation and surgery abroad.
  • Mental health support and continuity of treatment.
  • Medical evacuation or repatriation if local care is not appropriate.
  • Cover during school trips, holidays and travel back to Portugal.

Coordinate children and parents sensibly

The best solution may be a family international health plan, a dedicated student plan, or a combination of Portuguese cover for the parents and international cover for the child. The right answer depends on age, country, school requirements, medical history and how often the child travels.
