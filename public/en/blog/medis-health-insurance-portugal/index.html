What Médis offers

Médis is built around a large in-network model: you visit affiliated clinics and hospitals and pay only a small copayment, while Médis settles the rest directly with the provider. For day-to-day healthcare in Portugal, this is convenient and predictable.

Key strengths

  • Largest in-network in Portugal — agreements with a very wide range of clinics, hospitals, laboratories and pharmacies, including outside the big cities.
  • Low copayments — routine consultations are typically a few euros in-network, keeping everyday care affordable.
  • Strong dental and optical — available from mid-tier plans with good sublimits.
  • Family-friendly — competitive family pricing with paediatric and maternity cover well integrated.
  • Brand recognition — providers know Médis and accept it without friction.

Points expats should check

  • Limited international cover — Médis is designed for use within Portugal; cover abroad is minimal or emergency-only.
  • Documentation in Portuguese — contracts and communications are typically in Portuguese, so having an English-speaking intermediary manage it matters.
  • Waiting periods and documentation — Médis often expects more complete Portuguese paperwork (NIF, proof of address) than APRIL or Allianz.

Who Médis is ideal for

Médis suits long-term residents and families who are settled in Portugal, value the convenience of a vast national network and low copayments, and do not need international cover. If you travel frequently, want English-language documentation or are still building your Portuguese paperwork, weigh it carefully against APRIL and Allianz.
