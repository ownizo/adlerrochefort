Art in the house is not automatically insured

A household contents limit is written for furniture and ordinary possessions. A collection — paintings, sculpture, watches, wine — needs to be scheduled, valued and placed with an insurer that will pay on that class of risk. The same lines exist in Portugal and in Spain. The wording does not travel with the piece unless someone asks it to.

What we analyse in a collection

Art, jewellery and watches are insured well only when the policy reflects how each piece is valued, kept and used. We analyse that before approaching the insurers whose underwriting suits the collection.

Valuation and agreed value

Who values the pieces, how often valuations are updated, and whether they are insured on an agreed value or on market value at the time of loss.

Scheduled and unscheduled items

Which pieces must be listed individually, and what limits apply to items that are not listed, per piece and in total.

Where the pieces are kept and worn

At home, in a safe, in a bank vault or when travelling: each location changes the conditions and has to be declared.

Partial loss, restoration and loss of value

How restoration is handled and whether a loss of value after repair is considered.

New acquisitions, loans and transport

How new pieces are added, and what applies to works lent to exhibitions or shipped between countries.

Security and prevention

Alarm, storage and transport conditions that the insurer may set, and how they fit the way the collection is actually used.

What we need to prepare a proposal

  • A list of the pieces with recent valuations or purchase invoices.
  • Where each piece is kept, and the security there.
  • Pieces worn or taken abroad, and how often.
  • Any loans, exhibitions or planned transport.
  • Your current policy, if there is one.

We assess the risk, analyse the conditions available and look for the protection that fits the collection. All cover is subject to the insurer’s acceptance of the risk and to the terms of the policy issued.

Household first, then schedule

Most Portuguese and Spanish home policies cap unspecified valuables. Above that cap the piece is underinsured or simply not covered. Agreed value, against a valuation, is a different contract from “contents up to a sub-limit”.

The technical note for Portugal is when a collection outgrows a household policy and how an art claim is settled — restoration, loss in value, transit. This page is the cross-border version: same products, two legal systems.

Ask for a quote — it takes under a minute

Tell us the essentials. We reply in writing and only ask for more if we need it. As each proposal is specific and requires a detailed analysis of the risk, response times may vary.

A piece that moves is a different risk

Transit between a Lisbon apartment and a house in Marbella is a gap if the wording never mentioned it. Storage during works, a loan to a gallery, a month on a yacht: each is a location the household policy may never have been told about. Say where each piece lives, and whether it moves.

Why independence matters on collections

A tied agent places what their insurer will take. Collections are often declined or restricted there. As an independent broker we go to markets that write art — including specialist insurers — and we do not have to force the piece onto an Allianz or Zurich household wording that was never built for it. We also do not pretend those names are always the answer. The risk decides.

Occupancy sits underneath the collection

If the house is also let, or left empty for months, the home wording under the collection changes. A refused household claim does not become a paid art claim because the painting was scheduled on top of a policy that was already wrong about how the house is used. Tell us both.

Frequently asked questions

Does my home insurance cover the paintings?

Only up to the unspecified-valuables limit in that wording, which is often far below the value of even one picture. Above the limit the piece needs to be scheduled, with a valuation the insurer will accept.

If I move a work between Portugal and Spain, is it still covered?

Only if the wording says so. Household contents usually assume the items stay at the address on the schedule. Transit, a second home, or storage needs to be declared.

Do I have to use the insurer who covers the building?

No. The building or the resort policy and the collection are separate contracts. We are not tied to the insurer the developer or the household insurer prefers.
