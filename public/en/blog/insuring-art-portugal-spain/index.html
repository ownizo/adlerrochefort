Art in the house is not automatically insured

A household contents limit is written for furniture and ordinary possessions. A collection — paintings, sculpture, watches, wine — needs to be scheduled, valued and placed with an insurer that will actually pay on that class of risk. We place the same lines in Portugal and in Spain.

Household first, then schedule

Most Portuguese and Spanish home policies cap unspecified valuables. Above that cap the piece is underinsured or excluded. Transit between a Lisbon apartment and a Marbella house is another gap if the wording never mentioned it.

The longer technical note is art on a household policy in Portugal. This page is the cross-border version: same products, two legal systems.

Why independence matters on collections

A tied agent places what their insurer will take. Collections are often declined or restricted there. As an independent broker we go to markets that write art — including specialists such as Hiscox — and we do not have to force the piece onto an Allianz or Zurich household wording that was never built for it.

What we need for a review

A simple inventory, approximate values, where each piece lives, and whether it moves. If the house is also let or left empty, say so — occupancy changes the home wording that sits underneath the collection.
