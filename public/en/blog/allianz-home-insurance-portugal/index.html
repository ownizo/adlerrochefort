Allianz home insurance in Portugal: an expat guide for 2026

August 2026 · Adler & Rochefort

Part of our complete guide to home insurance in Portugal for property owners. For a free comparison across Zurich, Allianz, Hiscox and Liberty Mutual, request a quote in 24 hours.

Allianz has one of the broadest home insurance ranges in Portugal, and for owners who want to hold several policies with one insurer it is usually the first place we look. The proposition is less about a single outstanding cover and more about breadth: a lot of optional modules, a genuinely useful assistance service, and a structure that lets you build the policy up rather than accept a fixed package.

What follows is how the proposition actually behaves for English-speaking owners in Portugal, including the parts that are inconvenient.

What Allianz offers on a Portuguese home

The base is the same multi-risk habitação architecture every Portuguese insurer uses — building, contents, water damage, storm, theft, glass, electrical damage, liability, assistance. Where Allianz differs is in how much sits above that base as electable cover, and in how far the assistance goes.

  • Tiered product versions — typically a basic, an intermediate and a comprehensive tier, so the same insurer covers a €90,000 apartment and a €700,000 villa without forcing either into the wrong shape.
  • Extensive home assistance — beyond the standard emergency plumber and locksmith, the higher tiers commonly include appliance breakdown help, IT and small-works assistance, key-holder callout and pest control.
  • Accidental damage as an elective cover — the one that pays when you put a foot through the shower tray or a contractor drills into a pipe, and the one most owners assume they already have.
  • Loss of rent and alternative accommodation — useful on both let properties and permanent homes made uninhabitable by a claim.
  • Specified items and higher valuables limits, garden and outdoor property, bicycles, and cover for equipment away from the home.
  • Legal protection — frequently overlooked, and disproportionately useful in a country where neighbour and condominium disputes are common and litigation is slow.

The practical consequence is that an Allianz schedule for a well-specified villa is longer than most, and a good deal of the value sits in lines the owner never reads. It is worth reading them.

Where Allianz is strong

Concentrating home, health and motor. This is the clearest advantage and the most underrated. Holding home, private health and car cover with one insurer usually produces a multi-policy discount, but the real benefit is administrative: one renewal calendar, one claims number, one broker conversation, and no argument between two insurers about which policy responds when a claim straddles both. For expat households running three or four policies, it removes a genuine annoyance.

The assistance is not a token cover. Most Portuguese policies include 24-hour assistance and most owners never use it. On Allianz’s higher tiers it is broad enough to be worth using — and for a second-home owner who is 2,000 kilometres away when the boiler fails, a service that will dispatch a tradesperson and let them in via a key-holder is worth more than a marginally lower premium.

Optional covers that are actually available. Several Portuguese insurers list accidental damage or garden cover in the general conditions and then decline to write them on a given property. Allianz’s optional modules are, in our experience, reliably obtainable, which makes it easier to build a policy that fits an unusual property without leaving the retail market.

Breadth of appetite. Allianz will look at properties — letting, mixed use, larger villas — that some retail insurers turn away at the quote stage. Terms may be loaded, but a loaded quote is a quote.

Points to check before you sign

1. Earthquake is an optional cover, and in the Algarve it matters. Fenómenos sísmicos is never included by default in Portugal, and Allianz is no exception. It has to be requested, it adds roughly 15–30% to the premium in the Algarve, and — the part owners miss — the excess is expressed as a percentage of the sum insured, not of the loss. A 5% excess on a €500,000 building is €25,000 before the insurer pays anything. Decide deliberately rather than by omission; our article on earthquake cover in the Algarve works through the numbers.

2. The security requirements attached to theft cover. Higher contents sums insured come with conditions — specified lock types, security doors or shutters, sometimes a monitored alarm with a maintenance contract. These sit in the condições particulares as conditions precedent, meaning that if they are not in place at the time of a burglary, the theft cover can be reduced or declined outright. Read them when the policy is issued, not after.

3. The declaration of how the property is used. Allianz rates permanent residence, second home and letting differently, and the rating is only as good as the declaration. A property described as a permanent home and let out for eight weeks a summer is rated wrongly and exposed on precisely the claims letting creates. Changing the declaration mid-term is straightforward and usually cheap. Not changing it is the expensive option.

How Allianz compares

Against Zurich the choice is genuinely close on a mainstream property: Zurich for wording clarity and second-home occupancy, Allianz for optional-cover breadth, assistance and multi-policy consolidation. Against Hiscox, Allianz is the retail generalist against a high-value specialist. Against Liberty Mutual, Allianz covers the standard risks and Liberty the ones outside standard appetite. Frequently asked questions

Does Allianz include earthquake cover on Portuguese home policies?
No. Fenómenos sísmicos is an optional cover with Allianz, as with every insurer in Portugal, and it has to be requested explicitly. In the Algarve it adds roughly 15% to 30% to the premium, and the excess is a percentage of the sum insured — commonly 2% to 10% — rather than a fixed euro figure. A great many Algarve homes have no seismic cover simply because nobody asked.
What security does Allianz require for theft cover?
It depends on the contents sum insured and the property. Above certain levels the policy carries conditions precedent — specified lock types, reinforced doors or shutters, and sometimes a monitored alarm under a maintenance contract. If those measures are not in place at the time of a burglary, the theft claim can be reduced or refused. The requirements are listed in the condições particulares and are worth reading on day one.
Can I hold my home, health and car insurance all with Allianz?
Yes, and it is one of the main reasons owners choose them. Consolidating usually produces a multi-policy discount, but the practical benefit is a single renewal calendar and a single claims route. It also removes the argument that arises when a claim touches two policies held with different insurers.
Does Allianz cover accidental damage in the home?
Not as standard — it is an elective cover, and one most owners assume they already have. It is what responds when a foot goes through the shower tray, a television is knocked over, or a contractor drills into a concealed pipe. On a well-finished property it is usually worth the additional premium.
Will Allianz insure a property I let out?
Often yes, provided the letting is declared and the policy rated for it. Occasional letting can normally be added to a residential policy; registered Alojamento Local needs the commercial guest liability written in. The declaration is what makes the difference — an undeclared let property is rated as something it is not, and the guest-related claims are exactly the ones a residential wording excludes.
Is Allianz more expensive than other insurers in Portugal?
On a like-for-like basis it is broadly competitive with Zurich on mainstream properties. Allianz schedules can look more expensive because they carry more optional modules — accidental damage, legal protection, enhanced assistance — that a cheaper quote simply omits. The comparison only means something once both schedules cover the same things.
