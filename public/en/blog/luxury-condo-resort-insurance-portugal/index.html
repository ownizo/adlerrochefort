Luxury condos and resort homes: where the building policy ends

On a gold-standard development the estate looks fully managed. The insurance is not. The association covers the shared fabric. You cover the interior, the contents, the way the home is actually used — lived in, let, empty or mixed — and any art inside it.

What the condominium or resort actually insures

A Portuguese condominium in horizontal property must carry fire cover on the common structure. Golf and marina resorts add grounds, gates, clubhouses and the operator’s own liability. That is real cover. It stops at the door of your unit. Upgraded kitchens, contents, jewellery, art and your personal liability sit outside it. The same line exists in Spain; the wording and the compulsory pieces change.

We already wrote the resort cut in detail in golf resort properties and the contents gap in what the condominium policy does not cover.

Mixed use is the claim that fails

Many of these homes are not one thing. Six weeks as a family house, three months on a rental programme, the rest empty. A policy written as a permanent residence can refuse a letting claim. A holiday-home wording can impose unoccupancy conditions you have never read. The review is occupancy first, premium second.

Same product lines in Spain as in Portugal. Different law, different occupancy rules, different claim path. One broker who can place both.
