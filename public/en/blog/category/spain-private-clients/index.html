Spain — Private Clients

International households in Spain often have several risks that have accumulated at different times: a high-value home, a second property, vehicles, valuables, travel and family liabilities. Those risks are not automatically made coherent by placing them with one insurer, and a gap in ownership, occupancy or valuation can matter more than the number of policies held.

These articles look at the coordination problem from the owner's side. We cover higher-value homes, contents and security, property held through companies, multiple residences and the questions to ask when reviewing separate policies together. The aim is a clearer risk picture and a defensible arrangement, rather than a generic package label.

Looking for a quote rather than a read? See our Private client insurance in Spain page.
