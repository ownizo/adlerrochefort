Property Insurance in Spain

Owning property in Spain — a full-time home, a second home used seasonally, or a rental — raises different insurance questions depending on how the property is actually used. A comunidad de propietarios building policy does not cover your own contents; a non-resident owner is not automatically harder to insure; a property let to guests is usually not covered by a standard home policy at all.

These guides are written specifically for Spain — nothing here is adapted from our Portuguese content, and nothing here states a nationwide rule where one genuinely varies by region.
