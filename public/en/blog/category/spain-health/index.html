Health Insurance in Spain

Private health insurance in Spain sits alongside a broad public system rather than replacing it — the practical questions are what a specific plan actually includes, how it treats pre-existing conditions, and what a visa or residence application genuinely requires, which varies by route and is not something a general guide can state as one rule.

These guides are written specifically for Spain — nothing here is adapted from our Portuguese content, and nothing here names a Spanish insurer or promises a specific outcome for a visa application.
