Marine

A boat in Portugal is regulated as a vessel first and insured as one second, and the two sets of rules interact. Registration, flag, the qualifications required of the person at the helm and the permitted use of the vessel all feed into what an underwriter will write and on what terms. An owner arriving with a boat already insured elsewhere frequently finds that the existing policy does not follow the vessel into Portuguese registration, or does not follow it into Portuguese waters for more than a fixed number of weeks.

This category covers the practical stages of that. Importing and registering a boat here, and what has to be in place before the paperwork will complete. The distinction between private use and charter, which is the single most important declaration on a yacht policy: carrying paying guests is a different risk, a different legal regime and, on most wordings, an outright exclusion unless it has been declared and rated.

We also cover the seasonal mechanics that generate claims. Lifting, lay-up and winter storage are periods when cover often changes shape — some risks fall away, others such as fire and theft ashore become more relevant, and the lifting operation itself can sit in a gap between the yard's liability and the owner's policy. Berthing in the Algarve marinas raises its own questions about third-party liability to neighbouring vessels and about what the marina's own cover does and does not extend to.
