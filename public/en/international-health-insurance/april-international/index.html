International health insurance · APRIL International

APRIL International health insurance for expats

MyHealth International is the international health plan of APRIL International, built for people who live abroad for more than twelve months. We are an EU-regulated broker appointed by APRIL International: we configure the plan for where you actually live, handle the medical application and stay with you at claim time. Four details are enough to start.

Key facts

  • Four plans: Explore, Essential, Extensive and Elite, each with hospital treatment, cancer treatment and medical evacuation and repatriation included.
  • Annual limit per person: €/US$500,000 on Explore, €/US$1,500,000 on Essential, unlimited on Extensive and Elite.
  • Area of cover: worldwide excluding the United States, or worldwide including the United States.
  • For people living outside their country of nationality. Age at entry: 10 to 74 in most countries; 16 to 60 if you live in Brazil, Chile, China, Costa Rica, Hong Kong, Japan, Mexico, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, the UAE, the UK or the US.
  • Deductible: none, or €/US$500, 1,000, 2,500 or 5,000; out-patient co-payment of 0%, 10%, 20% or 30%.
  • Health cover insured by Groupama Gan Vie; assistance and personal liability by Chubb European Group SE. No fee to you.

How the plan is built

  • Hospital only, or hospital and out-patient. Out-patient adds GP and specialist consultations, pharmacy, tests, physiotherapy and preventive care.
  • Options: dental and optical; maternity on Essential, Extensive and Elite.
  • Area of cover: worldwide including the United States, or worldwide excluding it. Without the US, emergencies there are still covered during temporary stays of up to 60 consecutive days. Accidents and emergencies outside your area of cover are covered for stays of up to 60 days.
  • Deductible or co-payment: an annual deductible of €/US$500 to 5,000, or a co-payment of 10%, 20% or 30% on out-patient care, lowers the premium.
  • Extras: comprehensive repatriation assistance with personal liability in private life, a crisis assistance programme and travel incident cover.
  • French nationals can take the plan as a top-up to the Caisse des Français de l’Étranger (CFE) instead of from the first euro.

Premiums are payable in euro or US dollars: annually, half-yearly, quarterly or, in euro by SEPA direct debit, monthly.

Medical underwriting and waiting periods

You complete a health questionnaire, signed no more than three months before the start date. APRIL International may accept you on standard terms, accept you with specific conditions, or decline. Conditions excluded when you apply stay excluded, so declaring everything is what protects a future claim.

  • 3 months for routine dental care.
  • 6 months for major dental care and optical.
  • 12 months for maternity, genetic testing and preventive cancer surgery.
  • 24 months for medically assisted reproduction.

Except for maternity, waiting periods can be waived if you held equivalent cover that ended less than a month before. Planned treatment needs prior approval; without it, a 50% reduction applies even when the treatment is later accepted as necessary.

Using the plan day to day

  • Direct billing for hospital stays, with no upfront payment.
  • Easy Claim app for claims, hospital pre-approval and messages to your advisers; reimbursements by instant transfer in the SEPA area.
  • Easy Pay Card, a digital payment card issued on request for costly out-patient care such as scans, pharmacy or optical.
  • Telehealth with Teladoc Health, 24/7, free and unlimited, and a second medical opinion service.
  • More than 2 million healthcare professionals worldwide; the hospital network depends on your plan and country.

APRIL International, Allianz Care or Cigna Global

We are appointed by all three and quote them on the same assumptions. APRIL International often fits when you want to choose a precise level of hospital cover and a deductible of up to €/US$5,000, when you are French and contribute to the CFE, or when you want direct billing and a payment card for costly out-patient care. Its entry ages are stricter in some countries, so we check them first. For US citizens we configure Cigna Global; for a Europe-only area of cover, Allianz Care.

Frequently asked questions

Who insures APRIL International MyHealth International?

The health cover is insured by Groupama Gan Vie, and the assistance and personal liability benefits by Chubb European Group SE. APRIL International Care France designs and manages the plan.

Are pre-existing conditions covered?

Not automatically. You complete a health questionnaire and APRIL International sets your terms: standard acceptance, specific conditions or a decline. Conditions excluded when you apply stay excluded.

Is the United States covered?

Only if you choose worldwide cover including the United States. With worldwide cover excluding the US, emergencies there are covered during temporary stays of up to 60 consecutive days.

Is maternity covered?

As an option on Essential, Extensive and Elite, after a 12-month waiting period: €/US$4,000, 8,000 or 12,000 per pregnancy, with higher limits if there are complications at birth.

Up to what age can I join?

Up to 74 in most countries, and up to 60 if you live in Brazil, Chile, China, Costa Rica, Hong Kong, Japan, Mexico, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, the UAE, the UK or the US. Children can join below the minimum age on a parent’s policy.
