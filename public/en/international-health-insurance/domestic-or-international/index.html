Domestic or international health insurance?

The answer depends on where you will live, your nationality, your age and where you want to be treated, not on price. This guide applies the same rules we use when we prepare a proposal.

Key facts

  • International plans are for people living outside their country of nationality.
  • US citizens: Cigna Global. Allianz Care does not accept them.
  • In Spain we place international plans only.
  • Portuguese domestic plans: NIF and address in Portugal; new members over 65 only with MGEN, for two or more people.

How the guide decides

  • Nationality and residence. International plans cover people living outside their country of nationality. A Portuguese national living in Portugal is a domestic-plan client.
  • Where you want to be treated. Domestic plans cover treatment in Portugal. If you want cover in your home country or worldwide, the plan is international.
  • Age. Portuguese domestic plans generally do not accept new members over 65. MGEN does, for households of two or more people.
  • Documents. Domestic plans require a Portuguese tax number (NIF) and a residential address in Portugal. International plans do not.
  • Spain. We place international plans only. For clients who mainly need cover in Spain and in their home country, Cigna Close Care is one option.

Why the guide does not ask about your health

We never collect clinical information through the website. If anyone has a pre-existing condition, it is the insurer that assesses it, during medical underwriting, after you have seen the proposal. Portuguese domestic plans generally exclude pre-existing conditions; MGEN accepts them after a 365-day waiting period.

Frequently asked questions

Are my answers stored?

No. The guide runs in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent unless you choose to fill in the quote form.

Can a US citizen take out Allianz Care?

No. For US citizens we quote Cigna Global, which accepts Americans living outside the United States.

I live in Portugal and I am Portuguese. Can I take out an international plan?

International plans are designed for people living outside their country of nationality. If you hold a second nationality, tell us in the form.

Is a domestic plan enough if I am over 65?

Generally, Portuguese domestic plans do not accept new members over 65. MGEN does, for households of two or more people. Otherwise, the alternative is an international plan, subject to medical underwriting.
