International health insurance · Stays under 12 months

Short-term international health insurance for stays under 12 months

A sabbatical, a university year abroad, an au pair placement, a Working Holiday Visa or a fixed-term assignment needs proper health cover for a defined period, not an annual expat plan. APRIL International’s online tool covers these four situations, and quotes made through it are linked to Adler & Rochefort as your broker, at no extra cost to you.

Key facts

  • Four options in one tool: travel and stays, student or au pair, Working Holiday Visa, and professional stays, all for less than 12 months.
  • Hospitalisation without upfront payment, medical repatriation assistance and personal liability in private life.
  • Designed for a fixed stay outside your country of nationality, with a start and an end date.
  • Area of cover chosen when you apply: worldwide including or excluding the United States.
  • 100% online application, with the insurance certificate issued immediately.
  • Designed by APRIL International Care France; medical expenses insured by Groupama Gan Vie, assistance and liability by Chubb European Group SE.

  • Your nationality and the country of your stay must be different.

Emergency or Comfort: the two levels for long stays

On APRIL International’s long-stay plan (MyTravel Cover, ages 10 to 79, stays of 3 to 12 months) you choose between two levels:

  • Emergency: emergencies and accidents only, medical expenses up to €250,000, out-patient care up to €750.
  • Comfort: emergencies, accidents and out-patient care, medical expenses up to €500,000, with unlimited 24/7 telehealth.
  • On both: emergency dental care up to €400 (doubled after an accident), repatriation assistance and personal liability.
  • Claims and the digital insurance card in the Easy Claim app; in an emergency APRIL contacts the hospital directly.

Figures from APRIL International’s published benefits for MyTravel Cover. The student and professional options have their own tables, shown in the tool before you pay.

When it fits, and when it doesn’t

These plans are built for people on the move. For the families we work with, they are useful in situations such as:

  • A son or daughter spending a semester or an academic year at a university abroad.
  • An au pair from another country joining your household in Portugal or Spain.
  • Parents or relatives spending a long season with you, outside their own country.
  • A sabbatical, a long journey or a round-the-world year.
  • A fixed-term assignment abroad, for you or a member of your team.

They are not the right plan if you live in Portugal or Spain, or if you are moving abroad for more than a year: they end on the date you choose. For a long-term move you need an annually renewable plan, such as APRIL International MyHealth International, Allianz Care or Cigna Global, or a domestic plan. Our guide to domestic or international health insurance explains the difference.

Certificates for visas and universities

The certificate is issued online as soon as you pay. Universities, host programmes and consulates each set their own requirements: minimum amounts, repatriation, dates covering the whole stay. Compare the certificate with the list you were given before you submit it; if a requirement is not met, choose another level or area of cover in the tool, or write to us.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take out a short-term plan if I live in Portugal or Spain?

Not as your health cover there. These plans are built for a fixed stay with an end date. If Portugal or Spain is where you live, you need an annually renewable plan, international or domestic, which we can arrange.

My child is going to study abroad. Which option?

Student / au pair, from 1 month to 1 year, as long as the destination is not their country of nationality. For courses longer than a year, ask us about a renewable plan.

Is the United States covered?

Yes, if you choose the area of cover including the United States when you apply. For students going to the US, APRIL states the plan is compatible with the J-1 visa application.

What if the stay goes beyond 12 months?

These plans run for up to 12 months, except Working Holiday Visas for Canada, which can run for 18 or 24 months. If you expect to stay longer, write to us before the end date so that long-term cover can be put in place.

Do I pay more by going through Adler & Rochefort?

No. The premium is the one APRIL International shows in its tool. We are paid by the insurer, and you have a broker to turn to.
