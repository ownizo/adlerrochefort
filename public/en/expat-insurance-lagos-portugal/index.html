Frequently asked questions

Do I need private health insurance as an expat in Portugal?

Residents can access the public health system (SNS), but many expats add private cover for faster appointments, English-speaking doctors and broader choice. We help you decide what makes sense for your situation.

Can you help if I don't speak Portuguese?

Yes — that's our specialty. We handle communication with insurers and explain every policy in plain English.

Do you only cover Lagos?

We serve clients across the Algarve and all of Portugal. Lagos, Luz and Portimão are areas we know especially well.
