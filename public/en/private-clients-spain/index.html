We work in English

We advise in English. Where a Spanish insurer's wording is issued in Spanish, we make sure you understand exactly what it says — limits, conditions and exclusions — before you rely on it. Every answer you receive from us is in writing.

One household, one review

A household with a main residence in Madrid, a house on the coast, a collection, two or three cars and a family to protect rarely has one insurance question. It has several, usually bought at different times, from different insurers, sometimes in different countries — and nobody has looked at how the limits, dates and exclusions fit together.

We review the whole household, place each risk where it is individually underwritten, and say plainly when a well-built standard policy is the better answer. Households that also live in Portugal keep the same adviser, and — where the insurer writes both locations — the same policy.

What we analyse in a high-value home

In a property of real value the right cover is a matter of analysis, not of choosing a product. We look at the house, the way it is used and what is inside it, and only then approach the insurers whose underwriting fits.

Rebuild cost and sums insured

How the cost of rebuilding is established, whether the sums insured are sufficient and whether the policy limits the indemnity in any way — including how underinsurance and the proportional rule would apply.

Fire, flood, storm, earthquake and water

Which natural and accidental perils are covered, on what terms, with which excesses, and what local exposure — coast, river, slope, seismic zone — means for acceptance.

Contents, art, jewellery and collections

Whether contents are insured at a realistic value, which items need to be scheduled, how they are kept and what limits apply per item and in total.

Gardens, pools, outbuildings and equipment

Walls, pools, annexes, garages, solar installations and outdoor equipment: what is part of the building sum and what needs to be declared separately.

Liability, staff and the way the house is run

Liability limits set against the real exposure — guests, domestic staff, contractors, management services — and the cover that each of them may need.

Special properties and ownership structures

Historic or listed houses, unusual construction, properties owned through a company, and several homes in Portugal and Spain that should be insured coherently.

What we need to prepare a proposal

  • Address, year of construction and gross area.
  • An estimate of the rebuild cost, which we review with you; for Portugal we use the APS SCRIM simulator as a reference.
  • Contents and valuables to insure, and how they are kept.
  • Security, occupancy and any claims in the last five years.
  • Your current policy, if there is one.

We assess the risk, analyse the conditions available and look for the protection that fits. All cover is subject to the insurer’s acceptance of the risk and to the terms of the policy issued.

What we analyse in a collection

Art, jewellery and watches are insured well only when the policy reflects how each piece is valued, kept and used. We analyse that before approaching the insurers whose underwriting suits the collection.

Valuation and agreed value

Who values the pieces, how often valuations are updated, and whether they are insured on an agreed value or on market value at the time of loss.

Scheduled and unscheduled items

Which pieces must be listed individually, and what limits apply to items that are not listed, per piece and in total.

Where the pieces are kept and worn

At home, in a safe, in a bank vault or when travelling: each location changes the conditions and has to be declared.

Partial loss, restoration and loss of value

How restoration is handled and whether a loss of value after repair is considered.

New acquisitions, loans and transport

How new pieces are added, and what applies to works lent to exhibitions or shipped between countries.

Security and prevention

Alarm, storage and transport conditions that the insurer may set, and how they fit the way the collection is actually used.

What we need to prepare a proposal

  • A list of the pieces with recent valuations or purchase invoices.
  • Where each piece is kept, and the security there.
  • Pieces worn or taken abroad, and how often.
  • Any loans, exhibitions or planned transport.
  • Your current policy, if there is one.

We assess the risk, analyse the conditions available and look for the protection that fits the collection. All cover is subject to the insurer’s acceptance of the risk and to the terms of the policy issued.

Family liability

Guests, domestic staff, a pool, a boat, children studying abroad: exposure grows with the household, and a standard liability limit rarely keeps pace. See family liability insurance in Spain for the detail.

  • Limits that match the householdFamily liability limits set to the household’s exposure, confirmed in writing.
  • Defence costsLegal defence costs are covered; whether they sit inside or on top of the limit depends on the wording, and we confirm it in writing.
  • Who is coveredThe household, including children studying away, and anyone looking after your pets occasionally; guests and domestic staff in connection with the residence.
  • Every residenceAs owner, tenant or occupier — in Portugal, in Spain, or wherever the family has a home.

Frequently asked questions

What does "private client" insurance mean here?

Cover written for homes, contents and valuables that a standard home policy was not designed to carry — rebuild costs above the retail market, items worth more than a single-article limit, several residences, collections — together with family liability and family protection, reviewed as one household.

Which insurers do you use in Spain?

Specialist private-client insurers that underwrite in Spain. We are not tied to any of them; the insurer proposed depends on the risk and is named in writing with the proposal.

Can my homes in Spain and Portugal sit on one policy?

Often, yes, where the insurer writes both locations: several residences, their contents and scheduled valuables under one policy with one renewal date. Where that is not possible, we align the policies so that limits, dates and conditions leave no gap between them.

Do you insure art, jewellery and collections in Spain?

Yes. Scheduled items can be insured on an agreed value; territorial scope and any excess on valuables depend on the wording, and we confirm them in writing. Items above a stated threshold need a recent valuation; we tell you exactly what the underwriter will ask for before anything is requested.

Is there a minimum value?

It depends on the insurer: each specialist insurer sets its own entry level for a permanent residence. In practice individually underwritten cover makes clear sense when a home’s rebuild cost is around one million euros or more, when contents include pieces above a standard single-article limit, or when the household has homes in more than one country. Below that, we will tell you if a well-built standard policy is the better answer.

Will you need detailed financial information?

No. We ask only what the underwriter needs for the specific risk — the property, the sums insured, valuations for scheduled items and the security in place. Never your overall wealth.
