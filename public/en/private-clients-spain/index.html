Private client insurance in Spain

High-value homes, art and collections, family liability and family protection in Spain — placed with insurers licensed there and with specialist high-net-worth capacity, to the same standard we apply in Portugal, by the same adviser.

One household, one review

A household with a main residence in Madrid, a house on the coast, a collection, two or three cars and a family to protect rarely has one insurance question. It has several, usually bought at different times, from different insurers, sometimes in different countries — and nobody has looked at how the limits, dates and exclusions fit together.

We review the whole household, place each risk where it is individually underwritten, and say plainly when a well-built standard policy is the better answer. Households that also live in Portugal keep the same adviser, and — where the insurer writes both locations — the same policy.

High-value homes

Above a certain rebuild cost a standard hogar policy stops working: the sum insured drifts, the regla proporcional cuts the claim, and walls, pools and gardens are capped at a token figure. The wordings we place for high-value homes in Spain start from a different point:

  • On-site appraisal and rebuild costFor higher-value homes the insurer carries out an on-site appraisal at no cost to you, to confirm the rebuild cost, advise on contents and valuables sums insured and recommend risk-management measures.
  • No proportional reductionOnce the recommended sums are accepted, the insurer waives average: a partial loss is paid in full, even if building costs have moved since.
  • Rebuild guaranteedAfter a total loss the house is rebuilt even if the cost exceeds the buildings sum insured — provided the appraisal's recommended sums were accepted.
  • Comparable accommodationAlternative accommodation to a comparable standard, including for pets and horses, for as long as the house cannot be lived in — not the few months typical of the retail market.
  • Grounds, walls and outbuildingsTrees, shrubs and lawns, boundary and retaining walls, pools, annexes and guest houses carried at their own sums insured — not capped at a token figure.
  • Water, gas and trace and accessFinding and repairing escapes of water, gas or heating oil with no separate sub-limit, and the water or oil that is lost.
  • Settlement your wayA cash settlement, or repair by the suppliers, craftsmen and restorers you choose, with no penalty either way.
  • Excess waived on large lossesAbove a set claim size the excess is waived altogether — precisely where it would otherwise weigh most.

For the mechanics every Spanish home policy shares — community cover, occupancy, non-resident owners — see home insurance in Spain.

Contents, art and collections

Contents insured against all risks, worldwide, without the sub-limits that hollow out standard cover — and art, jewellery, watches and wine scheduled on an agreed value:

  • Worldwide all-risksPersonal possessions covered against all risks — at home, travelling and at a second home — with no separate sub-limit for what you carry.
  • Contents above the sum insuredOnce the recommended sums are accepted, a claim can exceed the contents sum insured by a pre-agreed margin if the true value proves higher.
  • No sub-limits where it mattersAccidental damage and loss, theft from cellars and outbuildings, and garden furniture without the sub-limits that hollow out standard cover.
  • Agreed valueArt, jewellery, watches and collections scheduled at a value fixed at inception against a valuation — the figure paid after a total loss, with no argument about depreciation.
  • No excessValuables insured on an agreed or declared value carry no excess.
  • Loss in value after restorationWhere a piece is restored but loses market value, the difference is paid — and repairs carry no cost ceiling.
  • Protection against under-valuationIf a professionally valued piece proves worth more than its sum insured on the day of the loss, the policy pays beyond the agreed value within a set margin.

See insuring art in Portugal and Spain.

Family liability

Guests, domestic staff, a pool, a boat, children studying abroad: exposure grows with the household, and a standard liability limit rarely keeps pace.

  • Limits that match the householdFamily liability at limits of several million euros, worldwide.
  • Defence costs on topLegal defence costs are paid in addition to the limit, not eroded from it.
  • Who is coveredThe household, including children studying away, and anyone looking after your pets occasionally; guests and domestic staff in connection with the residence.
  • Every residenceAs owner, tenant or occupier — in Portugal, in Spain, or wherever the family has a home.

Family protection

Risks that follow people rather than buildings. In the private-client wordings we place, they sit inside the household policy:

  • Kidnap and extortionKidnap and ransom expenses for a family member, including specialist consultants and rewards for information.
  • Carjacking and home invasionSupport and compensation after carjacking, aggravated burglary, assault, road rage or air rage.
  • Threats and stalkingSecurity consultants, temporary relocation and legal support when a family member is threatened or stalked.
  • Cyberbullying and reputationA psychologist, a cyber-security consultant, a lawyer and, where needed, the cost of changing schools after repeated cyberbullying.
  • CounsellingProfessional support for the family after any of these events.

Collector and high-value cars on an agreed value, with marque repairers and one schedule for several vehicles — see car insurance in Spain. International health cover underwritten person by person for families that live across borders — see health insurance in Spain. Investment and rental property

Where a household holds one or more rental properties in Spain alongside its own homes, they are reviewed together — see landlord insurance in Spain. Letting changes the risk an insurer is being asked to carry, and it has to be declared.

Region by region

Where the house is in Spain changes what the policy has to answer — wind and salt on the coast, months standing empty, a boat on a mooring, a villa let for part of the summer, a townhouse inside a community of owners. Those local questions are covered region by region in our guide to insuring a high-value home in Spain.

Frequently asked questions

What does "private client" insurance mean here?

Cover written for homes, contents and valuables that a standard home policy was not designed to carry — rebuild costs above the retail market, items worth more than a single-article limit, several residences, collections — together with family liability and family protection, reviewed as one household.

Which insurers do you use in Spain?

Insurers licensed in Spain — Hiscox underwrites in both Portugal and Spain — and specialist high-net-worth capacity distributed in Spain. We are not tied to any of them; the insurer proposed depends on the risk and is named in writing with the proposal.

Can my homes in Spain and Portugal sit on one policy?

Often, yes, where the insurer writes both locations: several residences, their contents and scheduled valuables under one policy with one renewal date. Where that is not possible, we align the policies so that limits, dates and conditions leave no gap between them.

Do you insure art, jewellery and collections in Spain?

Yes. Scheduled items are insured on an agreed value, worldwide, with no excess on scheduled valuables. Items above a stated threshold need a recent valuation; we tell you exactly what the underwriter will ask for before anything is requested.

Is there a minimum value?

No formal threshold. In practice individually underwritten cover makes clear sense when a home’s rebuild cost is around one million euros or more, when contents include pieces above a standard single-article limit, or when the household has homes in more than one country. Below that, we will tell you if a well-built standard policy is the better answer.

Will you need detailed financial information?

No. We ask only what the underwriter needs for the specific risk — the property, the sums insured, valuations for scheduled items and the security in place. Never your overall wealth.
