For households with more to coordinate

A Spanish second home, rental property, a move from another country or several policies administered by an assistant can call for a joined-up review. You do not need an unusual collection or a minimum net worth to benefit from a clearer insurance picture.

A policy for each purchase is not a household plan

A bank-arranged policy, cover bought when you moved and a motor renewal may each reflect a different moment in your life. We look for outdated values, missing people, undeclared rental use and gaps between contracts before discussing changes.

What we coordinate

The review can bring together Spanish property, second-home and landlord requirements, health, motor, life and mortgage-related protection. Specialist assets and unusual exposures are assessed individually: we review the requirement and confirm what can be arranged.

Several homes, with country-appropriate policies

Non-resident ownership, long periods away, short-term letting and permanent occupation need different disclosures. If your household also has Portuguese interests, we coordinate the review across both countries without assuming one policy or insurer works in both. See the Portugal service.

High-value and non-standard Spanish property

We review rebuilding costs, ownership, occupancy, improvements and existing schedules. A higher purchase price does not establish the correct building sum insured. Unusual construction, a claims history or complex rental use needs insurer assessment; Portuguese specialist arrangements do not establish availability in Spain. High-value homes in Spain · Landlord insurance.

Art, jewellery, watches and collections

Tell us about items whose values exceed a standard contents limit, collections moving between residences or valuables used away from home. We review valuations, security and territorial requirements and confirm what can be arranged. We do not assume agreed value, worldwide cover, art restoration or wine spoilage cover is available for a Spanish risk.

Vehicles and marine requirements

For Spanish motor insurance, residence, registration, drivers and actual use form the starting point. Our Spain motor page explains the ordinary requirements. Collector vehicles and boats can be included in the review, but specialist availability and navigation or usage terms must be established separately.

Health, international health and family protection

We review local versus international treatment needs, residence and existing health cover. Acceptance and benefits depend on the insurer and product. Life and mortgage-related cover should reflect today’s family commitments rather than an old loan balance. Health · Life · Mortgage protection.

Family liability across everyday activities

We examine who is insured, the activities and countries involved, exclusions and limits under existing contracts. Property-owner liability is not necessarily a complete answer to the household’s personal liabilities. Any additional requirement is reviewed for availability.

Staff, letting and remotely managed homes

A property manager’s involvement does not remove an owner’s disclosure responsibilities. We ask about occupancy checks, keyholders, staff, rentals and building works, and identify where a separate employment, liability or works arrangement needs to be confirmed.

Managing insurance for a client or family?

A spouse or family member, Personal or Executive Assistant, Property, House or Estate Manager, Family Office or professional adviser can begin the review. Tell us your role and whether you are authorised to discuss the requirements. We agree the appropriate authority and information-sharing consent before discussing personal policy details; no documentary authority is requested on this first form.

How the review works

  1. Map the household. Tell us who owns what, where it is used and who administers the insurance. A broad outline is enough to begin.
  2. Read what is already in place. We review schedules, wordings, renewal dates, declared use, values and gaps with you.
  3. Confirm a practical plan. We identify priorities, explain what needs underwriting and confirm available options and conditions before you decide.

A review is not a promise of placement. Timing depends on the risk, documents and insurer response.

Renewals, valuations and claims support

A change of home, a new acquisition, building work or a move between countries can matter before renewal. We help review values and declared use, prepare renewal information and communicate changes to the insurer. If a loss occurs, we assist with preparing and communicating the claim and remain involved during the claims process. The insurer decides the claim under the policy terms.
