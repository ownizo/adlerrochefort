Insurance claims support

A broker's role does not necessarily end when a policy is issued. Here is what we do when a client needs to make a claim, and where our role honestly stops.

Our role

What we help with

We help clients understand the claims process, communicate with insurers and keep track of the case where the policy and circumstances allow us to assist. This is broker support, not a promise to handle every part of a claim ourselves.

  • Notify us where appropriate For urgent or emergency situations, follow the insurer's own emergency assistance line first if the policy has one — that is normally the fastest route to help. Tell us as soon as you reasonably can afterwards.
  • Insurer notification We help make sure the claim is notified to the insurer correctly and within any time limit the policy sets.
  • Documentation and evidence We tell you what the insurer typically asks for — photos, invoices, repair estimates, police or other official reports where relevant — so nothing obvious is missing when the file is submitted.
  • Communication with the insurer We act as a point of contact between you and the insurer during the assessment, in English, so you are not left decoding correspondence in another language on your own.
  • Following up We keep track of where the case stands and follow up where there are unreasonable delays.
Being honest about the limits

What we do not do

Adler & Rochefort is an insurance intermediary, not the insurer. The insurer decides whether a claim is covered and pays any settlement — not us.

We do not guarantee claim approval
Whether a claim is covered is assessed by the insurer against the policy wording and the actual circumstances.
We do not promise a settlement timeframe
How long assessment takes depends on the insurer, the complexity of the claim, and how quickly documentation is available — we do not quote a fixed number of days.
We do not guarantee full indemnity
What is paid, if anything, is set by the policy's own terms, limits and excesses.
We do not provide legal representation
For a disputed claim that needs formal legal action, that is a matter for a lawyer, not for a broker.
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee my claim will be approved?

No — claim outcomes are decided by the insurer against the policy wording and the circumstances, not by us. We help present the case clearly and communicate with the insurer on your behalf where the policy allows it.

Can you settle my claim or offer legal representation?

No. We are an insurance intermediary, not the insurer, a claims administrator or a law firm. Settlement is decided and paid by the insurer; for a disputed claim needing legal representation, that is a matter for a lawyer.

Do you help with claims for policies you did not sell me?

Our claims support applies to policies placed through Adler & Rochefort. Contact us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help with your specific case.

Need new cover instead? This page is for clients who already hold a policy with us and need help with a claim. If you are looking for new insurance, start an Insurance Review instead.

Write to us with your policy details and what has happened, and we will confirm the next step.
