What a business multi-risk policy is not, in Portugal

Multi-risk business insurance itself is not mandatory. What almost no company escapes is workplace accident insurance, required for every employee, with a separate regime for self-employed contractors. Outside the legal requirements, what actually determines your exposure is public liability — damage caused to third parties in the course of your activity — and whether your sums insured are adequate. An out-of-date sum insured triggers the proportional rule: a company insured for half the rebuild value gets half the payout, even on a partial claim.

Other exposures worth covering

  • Loss of profit. Compensates for lost gross margin and the fixed costs that keep running during a shutdown. The most overlooked cover, and the one that closes the most companies when it's missing.
  • Cyber risk. Ransomware, business interruption, incident response and data-breach notification. See our piece on cyber insurance for businesses.
  • Public liability. Errors and omissions in the service delivered. Required in several regulated professions and advisable in any consulting or project-based activity — see professional liability insurance if that's the risk you're covering.
  • D&O (Directors & Officers). Responds to management decisions that cause loss to the company, its shareholders or third parties. Read more about D&O insurance.
  • Electrical damage and machinery breakdown. Power surges and sudden failure of production equipment — often the most expensive uncovered loss in industry and hospitality.

Who this is for

Any company operating in Portugal — from a single office to a multi-site operation with employees, equipment and stock to protect. If your exposure is a construction project rather than ongoing operations, see construction works insurance instead.

Common mistakes

  • Copying the sum insured from a previous policy. Rebuild costs, equipment values and stock levels change; the figure on last year's renewal rarely still matches reality.
  • Treating public liability as an afterthought. It's often the largest uncovered exposure in a multi-risk review, not a minor add-on.
  • Assuming the landlord's building cover extends to you. In leased premises, the building is the landlord's responsibility, but fit-out, contents, equipment, stock and your own public liability are yours — a split that's frequently misunderstood in the lease itself.

Frequently asked questions

What insurance is mandatory for a company in Portugal?

Workplace accident insurance is mandatory for every employee. Additional covers are required depending on the activity — construction, transport, regulated activities — and motor insurance for company vehicles.

Is business multi-risk insurance itself mandatory?

No, unless required by a contract — for example by your landlord or a lender. It is, however, the policy that brings together the protection of your property and your public liability in one place.

What is the proportional rule and why does it matter?

If your sum insured is lower than the real value of the asset, the payout is reduced in the same proportion. Half the sum insured means half the payout, even on a small partial claim.

We operate from leased premises. What do we need to insure?

The building itself is the landlord's responsibility, but improvements, contents, equipment, stock and your own public liability are the company's. This split is often poorly addressed in the lease agreement.

Do we need cyber risk cover?

If your company handles personal data, invoices through IT systems, or depends on them to operate, the exposure exists. GDPR requires data-breach notification within 72 hours, and the cost of incident response is rarely just the ransom itself.

How often should we review our policies?

Annually, and whenever there's significant investment, a change of premises, a change of activity or a meaningful change in headcount.

How long does a review take?

We come back with a first opinion in 48 to 72 business hours. A full review of a portfolio with several policies usually takes a few days more, depending on the documentation available.
