Private Office · Portugal & Spain

Adler & Rochefort Private Office

Insurance advisory for families, residences and collections across Portugal and Spain.

Adler & Rochefort is an insurance intermediary registered with Portugal’s insurance supervisor, the ASF (no. 425591790/3), authorised to operate in Spain and not tied to any insurer. Its Private Office looks after the whole insurance picture of international families — homes, collections, vehicles, health, liability and personal security — with one adviser, written recommendations and conditions agreed with specialist insurers before anything happens.

We work quietly, in writing and for the long term. Most of our clients came to Portugal or Spain from elsewhere; many hold property, collections or family members in more than one country. What they ask of us is simple: that the cover matches what they actually own and how they actually live, and that someone answers for it when it matters.

How we work

Our standard

  1. 01

    Discretion

    We ask only for what an underwriter needs to assess the risk — never for a statement of total wealth. Files, valuations and inventories stay with the people who need them.

  2. 02

    Independence

    We are not bound to any insurer. Each recommendation names the insurer, the conditions and the reasons, and we say so when a standard policy is the better answer.

  3. 03

    Substance before price

    We do not take part in price races. A proposal is built on the cover that protects the asset: rebuild value rather than market value, agreed value for collections, no proportional reduction where the market allows it.

  4. 04

    Continuity

    The same adviser from the first conversation to the renewal and, above all, at a claim. Sums insured are reviewed every year, because values move and an unrevised policy drifts.

  5. 05

    One view across borders

    Portugal and Spain are handled together, and international health cover follows the family wherever it lives.

Engagement

How an engagement works

  1. A private conversation

    By video call, by phone or in person in Lisbon or the Algarve: how the family lives, what it owns and where.

  2. A review of what is in place

    Existing policies read clause by clause: sums insured, per-item limits, exclusions and the gaps between policies.

  3. A written recommendation

    Within 24–72 working hours of having the information: insurers approached, conditions obtained, what changes and why.

  4. Placement and stewardship

    Policies placed, values reviewed every year, and the same adviser beside you at a claim.

Questions

What is the Adler & Rochefort Private Office?

The Private Office is how Adler & Rochefort, an ASF-registered insurance intermediary not tied to any insurer, looks after international families in Portugal and Spain: one adviser for homes, collections, vehicles, health, liability and personal security, with written recommendations and conditions agreed with specialist insurers in advance.

Who is it for?

Families whose homes, collections or circumstances need cover written for them rather than taken off the shelf — several residences, valuable contents, family members living in different countries. Where a well-built standard policy is the better answer, we say so in writing.

Does Adler & Rochefort work in Spain as well as Portugal?

Yes. The firm is authorised to operate in Spain under the EU freedom to provide services, and where an insurer writes both countries, a household’s properties in Portugal and Spain can sit on a single policy.

How is the service paid for?

There is no fee to the client: as an insurance intermediary, Adler & Rochefort is remunerated by the insurer through the commission included in the premium.

Is information kept confidential?

Yes. We ask only for what underwriting requires, handle personal data under the GDPR, and never ask for a client’s total net worth.

In which languages do you advise?

In writing, in English, Portuguese, Spanish and German.
