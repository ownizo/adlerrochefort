For households with more to coordinate

Several residences, a home undergoing renovation, a collection that travels, family health cover or a property managed while you are abroad: complexity does not begin at a particular net worth. This service is for households whose insurance needs to be understood as a whole.

Separate policies can leave connected risks apart

Policies bought at different times can disagree about ownership, occupancy, values or who is insured. A generous contents total will not solve a restrictive single-item limit. A home policy may not follow a collection in transit. We start with these connections, then decide what each contract needs to do.

What we coordinate

Property and contents; art, jewellery, watches and wine; vehicles and marine; health and international health; family liability; staff and property management. Life and mortgage-related protection can also form part of the household review. Coordination does not require every risk to sit with one insurer.

Several residences, sometimes several countries

We map each home’s ownership, use, occupation and management arrangements. Holiday letting, long empty periods and renovation must be declared separately. A household with Portuguese and Spanish interests can have one coordinated review, while each policy remains appropriate to its country. See the Spain service.

High-value and non-standard homes

Rebuilding value is not purchase value. Quality of finishes, traditional construction, pools, gardens, retaining walls and outbuildings all affect the schedule. Underinsurance can reduce a partial settlement proportionally; any waiver of average or tolerance must be confirmed in the wording. Earthquake cover and its excess need an explicit decision.

For individually underwritten Portuguese risks, the existing technical library explains Hiscox and Liberty Mutual through Innovarisk, and the role of specialist underwriting. Availability depends on the actual risk. Our choice to work with selected insurers is a service choice, not a regulatory restriction on market access. How specialist placement works.

Art, jewellery, watches and collections

Agree the basis of value before a loss. We review valuations, single-item limits, scheduled items, security requirements and possessions away from home. For art, restoration costs and residual loss in value matter alongside total loss; transit, loans and storage need explicit terms. For wine, discuss the inventory, valuation basis, storage conditions and spoilage.

Agreed value, worldwide cover and restoration provisions are policy features to confirm, not automatic benefits. Valuations · Jewellery and watches · Wine cellars.

Collector vehicles and boats

For collector or high-value cars, review agreed value, use, storage and repair arrangements. For boats and yachts, the questions include hull value, navigation limits, marina liability requirements, crew, private use versus charter and lay-up. A household review identifies these requirements; each placement needs its own underwriting. Collector cars · Marine guide.

Health that follows your actual residence

Local and international health insurance answer different needs. We review residence, treatment geography, existing cover and continuity before looking at options. Eligibility, exclusions and medical underwriting are confirmed separately. Do not send medical records at first contact. Health insurance in Portugal · International health insurance.

Family liability deserves its own discussion

Who is insured, where liability applies and which activities are excluded can matter more than the headline limit. Overseas expectations — including US liability expectations — may differ from Portuguese policy limits. Family liability guide · US buyer expectations.

Household staff and property management

Tell us who employs staff, who holds keys and who checks the property during absences. Employment-related cover, owner liability and a manager’s own professional responsibilities are different questions. Renovation also changes the risk before the home is occupied. Domestic staff guide.

Managing insurance for a client or family?

A spouse or family member, Personal or Executive Assistant, Property, House or Estate Manager, Family Office or professional adviser can begin the review. Tell us your role and whether you are authorised to discuss the requirements. We agree the appropriate authority and information-sharing consent before discussing personal policy details; no documentary authority is requested on this first form.

How the review works

  1. Map the household. Tell us who owns what, where it is used and who administers the insurance. A broad outline is enough to begin.
  2. Read what is already in place. We review schedules, wordings, renewal dates, declared use, values and gaps with you.
  3. Confirm a practical plan. We identify priorities, explain what needs underwriting and confirm available options and conditions before you decide.

A review is not a promise of placement. Timing depends on the risk, documents and insurer response.

Renewals, valuations and claims support

A change of home, a new acquisition, building work or a move between countries can matter before renewal. We help review values and declared use, prepare renewal information and communicate changes to the insurer. If a loss occurs, we assist with preparing and communicating the claim and remain involved during the claims process. The insurer decides the claim under the policy terms.
