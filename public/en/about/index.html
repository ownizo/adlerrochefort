Why Adler & Rochefort

You built a life somewhere else. Now protect it here.

Deciding to live in Portugal or Spain is rarely only about the weather. It is about moving a home, a family, a business, sometimes a collection built over decades — into a legal and insurance system written in another language.

Adler & Rochefort is an English-speaking insurance agency. We read the policy before we recommend it, and we tell you plainly where the cover stops.

Regulated scope

Licensed in two countries

Adler & Rochefort holds insurance intermediary authorisation in Portugal, granted by the Autoridade de Supervisão de Seguros e Fundos de Pensões (ASF). That authorisation is passported into Spain under Freedom of Services — Livre Prestação de Serviços, or LPS.

In practice this means one adviser can arrange and service cover on both sides of the border. If you own a house in the Algarve and an apartment in Andalusia, or you are moving from one country to the other, the file does not have to be handed over to a stranger halfway through.

Among brokers this is uncommon. Most are authorised in a single country, and that is the honest reason we mention it.

What independence means in practice is narrower and more useful than it sounds. It means the recommendation is not tied to a single brand, and not tied to a bank that sold you a mortgage and attached a policy to it. If two insurers would treat your risk differently — an empty house in winter, a pre-existing condition, a boat that is sometimes chartered — we can say which one, and why.

It also means we can be wrong out loud. Where none of these insurers will write the risk properly, we say so rather than selling you the nearest approximation.
